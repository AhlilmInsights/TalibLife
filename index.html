Umm al-Qura method

Calculated on your phone with the Umm al-Qura method, so it works offline. In Saudi Arabia ʿIshāʾ is 90 min after Maghrib (120 in Ramaḍān). If your mosque or al-Moṣally differs by a minute or two, adjust it below.

Muṣḥaf print

All three are the King Fahd Complex Madinah Muṣḥaf (Ḥafṣ), 604 pages, 15 lines. To tell them apart, look at page 76: if it ends with the title of Sūrat an-Nisāʾ, your copy is the 1421 or 1405 print; if an-Nisāʾ starts at the top of page 77, it is the 1440–1441 print. Then page 586: at-Takwīr at the top means 1405. Line progress is kept separately for each print.
